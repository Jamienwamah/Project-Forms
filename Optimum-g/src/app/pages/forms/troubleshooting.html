EMPLOYEE CONSENT & TERMS AND CONDITIONS

I hereby authorize the IT Department of Optimum Global Financial Services Limited to perform the necessary troubleshooting, diagnostics, maintenance, and repair activities on the above-mentioned company-issued device.

I understand that the troubleshooting process may include:

  • Hardware diagnostics and testing
  • Software troubleshooting and repair
  • Operating system updates or reinstallation
  • Driver installation or updates
  • Malware or virus scanning and removal
  • Configuration changes
  • Password resets (where applicable)
  • Temporary removal and reinstallation of business applications
  • Replacement of faulty hardware components (where approved)

I acknowledge that although the IT Department will make every reasonable effort to preserve existing data, there may be situations where data loss is unavoidable during troubleshooting or repair.

TERMS AND CONDITIONS

  1. Authorization: I authorize the IT Department to access and troubleshoot my assigned company device for the purpose of diagnosing and resolving reported issues.
  2. Data Backup: Where possible, I understand that IT will attempt to back up important business data before carrying out major repairs. However, I acknowledge that data recovery cannot always be guaranteed.
  3. Software Installation: I authorize IT to install, update, remove, or reconfigure software required to restore the device to normal operation.
  4. Hardware Repair: Where necessary and approved, defective hardware components may be repaired or replaced.
  5. Security Compliance: IT may review system configurations, installed applications, and security settings to ensure compliance with company policies.
  6. Temporary Device Retention: I understand that the device may need to remain with the IT Department for the duration of troubleshooting and repair.
  7. Confidentiality: The IT Department will maintain the confidentiality of business information accessed during troubleshooting and will only access files where necessary to resolve the reported issue.
  8. Reporting Additional Issues: If unrelated hardware or software faults are discovered during diagnosis, the IT Department may perform additional corrective actions or seek further approval where required.
